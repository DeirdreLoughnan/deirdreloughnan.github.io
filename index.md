# Deirdre Loughnan, PhD

![](/images/Loughnan_Profile_Pic.jpeg)

I am currently the Sentinels of Change data synthesis postdoctoral research fellow with the Hakai Research Institute, hosted in the O'Connor Lab at the University of British Columbia (UBC). I am interested in studying the effects of climate change on the drivers of community assembly and species interactions across temporal and spatial scales. I completed my PhD in Forest and Conservation Sciences at UBC, where I studied the impacts of climate change on the timing of life history events or phenology, with a particular focus on how environmental cues shape the timing of spring leafout across North American temperate forests. Previously I studied the biogeography of plant-insect interactions, community assembly of tall grass prairies, and pollination biology in alpine ecosystems.
